Who uses the most electricity and water — and where data centres actually sit

Data centres are 4.7% of US electricity and roughly 0.015% of US water withdrawals — small in the total, but the fastest-growing single load on the grid. Hover any bar for the exact figure, the share it represents and the source. Use the tabs and toggles to change the view.

Electricity

US retail power sales were about 4,080 TWh in 2025. Data centres are not a sector in the official statistics — EIA books them inside “commercial” and “industrial”.

Water

The US withdraws about 322 billion gallons a day. Power plants and irrigation take 90% of it. “Withdrawal” is water taken in; “consumption” is the part not returned.

So are data centres driving up the bill?

Bills move mainly through capacity and network costs, not through the servers’ own kilowatt-hours. This is what happened to PJM’s capacity price — the largest US grid — as forecast peak load grew “mainly from data centers”.
